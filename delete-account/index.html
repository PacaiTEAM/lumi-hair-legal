Delete your account

Effective: September 19, 2026 · Document version: 2026-09-19

Delete your account in the app

Open Lumi Hair and go to Profile → Delete Account. Review the deletion and subscription information, complete any requested verification, and confirm that you want to permanently delete your account.

Request deletion without the app

If you cannot access the app, email lumihair.help@pacaitechnologies.com with the subject “Delete my Lumi Hair account” and include the email address associated with your account. You do not need to reinstall the app.

We may ask you to verify that you own the account before completing the request. Never send us your password, payment-card information, or photo identification unless we specifically explain why additional verification is legally necessary.

What we delete

Deleting your account removes your Lumi Hair account and associated application data from our active systems. This includes your account profile, saved and dismissed hairstyle choices, Hair Profile photos, generated previews, generation records, and policy-acceptance records.

Information that may remain

Some information may remain temporarily in restricted backups, security logs, or service-provider systems until it is deleted or overwritten under the applicable retention schedule. We may also retain information when reasonably necessary to comply with legal, tax, accounting, fraud-prevention, security, or dispute-resolution obligations.

Apple and our subscription provider may separately retain purchase, transaction, and subscription records according to their legal obligations and retention policies. For more information, review our Privacy Policy.

Subscriptions

Deleting your Lumi Hair account or uninstalling the app does not automatically cancel or refund an App Store subscription. To prevent future renewal charges, manage or cancel your subscription through your Apple Account subscription settings.

Deletion timing

In-app deletion begins after you confirm the request. Deletion requests submitted by email are normally completed within 30 days after we verify account ownership. Certain backup copies and records that we are legally required to retain may remain for longer, as described above.
