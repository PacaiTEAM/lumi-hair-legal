Who we are

Lumi Hair is operated by Pacai Technologies LLC. This policy explains how we handle information through the Lumi Hair app and our legal website. Contact us at lumihair.help@outlook.com.

Information we collect

We collect information you provide when creating an account, including your email address and authentication credentials. We also store your saved and dismissed hairstyle choices, photos you select or take for your Hair Profile, generated hairstyle previews, and information about preview requests and usage allowances. If you contact support, we receive the information you send us.

When you use Lumi+, our subscription providers process purchase, transaction, and entitlement information associated with your account. Our service providers also process technical information needed to operate and secure their services, such as network requests and diagnostic records. We record the versions of the Terms and Privacy Policy presented when you accept, your confirmation that you are at least 18, the time of acceptance, and your AI photo permission for each new generation.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate you, operate your account, remember hairstyle choices, store your Hair Profile, generate requested previews, manage subscription access, respond to support requests, and maintain service reliability and security. We use acceptance and permission records to document your choices.

Photos and AI previews

You choose which photos to provide using your camera or photo library. Uploading a Hair Profile stores those photos privately with our hosting provider. Uploading alone does not request AI generation.

When you explicitly request an AI preview, we send your front and back photos, hairstyle reference images, and generation instructions to OpenAI. A generated front preview may also be sent to OpenAI to create the matching back preview. These images may show your face, hair, body, clothing, and background. AI previews are returned to Lumi Hair and stored with your account.

You can decline AI processing and continue using features that do not require it. Declining future requests does not reverse processing already started.

Providers and disclosures

We use Supabase for authentication, database services, and private image storage; OpenAI for requested image generation; and RevenueCat with the applicable app store for subscription management. RevenueCat receives your Lumi Hair account identifier to associate purchases with your account.

We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to valid legal requests, prevent fraud or abuse, or protect people and the service.

AI training and provider retention

OpenAI states that API data is not used to train its models unless the API customer opts in. Its default abuse-monitoring retention is generally up to 30 days, with exceptions for legal and safety reasons. [VERIFIED OPENAI TRAINING AND RETENTION SETTINGS]

How long we keep information

Account information, hairstyle choices, Hair Profile photos, and generated previews remain associated with your account until deletion. Replacing a photo currently retains earlier private photo versions and previews; individual photo or preview deletion is not currently available.

Deleting your account removes its private media and associated application records, including legal acceptance records, from our active Supabase systems. Your choices and requests

You can change supported account details, remove saved hairstyles, control camera and photo permissions through your device, and delete your account through Profile → Delete Account. You can also request account deletion or assistance at lumihair.help@outlook.com. We may verify account ownership before acting; never send us your password.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, obtain a copy of, or delete personal information, and to appeal a denied request. Contact us to exercise applicable rights. We will respond within legally required periods.

Deleting your account or uninstalling the app does not cancel an app-store subscription. Manage cancellation through the store where you subscribed.
