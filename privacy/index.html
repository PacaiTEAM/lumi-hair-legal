Privacy Policy

Effective: September 19, 2026 · Document version: 2026-09-19

Who we are

Lumi Hair is operated by Pacai Technologies LLC. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, retain, and protect information through the Lumi Hair mobile application and our legal website.

If you have questions or would like to make a privacy request, contact us at lumihair.help@outlook.com .

Information we collect

Account information

When you create an account, we collect your email address, an account identifier, and authentication information needed to sign you in. We do not have access to your plaintext password.

Hairstyle activity

We store information about hairstyles you save or dismiss, your hairstyle preferences, preview requests, generated previews, and usage allowances associated with your account.

Photos

If you create a Hair Profile, we collect the front and back photos that you choose to take or select. Depending on the photos you provide, they may include your face, hair, body, clothing, and background.

Purchases and subscriptions

If you purchase Lumi+, Apple and RevenueCat process information about the purchase, such as your subscription product, purchase status, renewal status, transaction or receipt information, and entitlement. RevenueCat also receives your Lumi Hair account identifier so that we can associate your subscription with your account. We do not receive your complete payment-card information.

Consent and policy records

We record the versions of the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy presented when you accept them, the time of acceptance, your confirmation that you are at least 18, and your AI photo-processing permission for each new generation request.

Support communications

If you contact us, we receive the information included in your message and any files or other information you choose to provide.

Technical and advertising information

Lumi Hair and its service providers may automatically process technical information necessary to operate, secure, and improve their services. This may include your device type, operating system, app version, IP address, network information, request timestamps, diagnostic information, advertising identifier when available, and information about ad impressions and interactions.

How we use information

We use information to:

  • create, authenticate, and maintain your account;
  • remember your saved and dismissed hairstyles;
  • store and manage your Hair Profile;
  • generate AI hairstyle previews that you request;
  • provide and manage Lumi+ subscription benefits;
  • display, measure, and protect against fraud involving ads;
  • enforce usage limits and prevent misuse;
  • respond to support and privacy requests;
  • maintain the reliability and security of Lumi Hair; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

Photos and AI previews

If you create a Hair Profile, we privately store the photos you choose to provide. When you request an AI preview, we send your photos, hairstyle reference images, generation instructions, and, when needed, generated previews to OpenAI to create the requested results. Generated previews are returned to Lumi Hair and stored with your account.

We do not use your photos or generated previews for advertising, sell them, or use them for facial recognition or biometric identification. AI processing is optional, and you may continue using features that do not require it.

Service providers and disclosures

We disclose information to service providers only as reasonably necessary to operate Lumi Hair:

  • Supabase provides authentication, database services, backend infrastructure, and private image storage.
  • OpenAI processes photos, hairstyle references, and instructions when you request an AI hairstyle preview.
  • RevenueCat processes subscription, transaction, entitlement, device, and account-identifier information used to manage Lumi+.
  • Apple processes App Store purchases, subscription payments, refunds, and related transaction information.
  • Google AdMob displays and measures advertisements and processes technical and advertising information as described below.
  • GitHub hosts our public legal website through GitHub Pages.

We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, respond to valid legal process, investigate fraud or abuse, enforce our Terms of Use, protect the security of Lumi Hair, or protect the rights and safety of our users or others.

If Pacai Technologies LLC is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, bankruptcy, or sale of assets, information may be disclosed as part of that transaction, subject to applicable law.

OpenAI processing and retention

OpenAI states that data submitted through its API is not used to train or improve its models unless the API customer explicitly opts in. We have not opted in to sharing Lumi Hair API data for model training.

Under OpenAI’s standard API settings, image-generation and image-editing requests may be retained in abuse-monitoring logs for up to 30 days. OpenAI may retain information longer when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect its services or third parties from harm. Images submitted to OpenAI may also be scanned for child sexual abuse material and may be retained for review if potentially unlawful content is detected.

Advertising and tracking

Lumi Hair uses Google AdMob to display and measure advertisements. AdMob may receive information such as your IP address, device and operating-system information, app identifier, advertising identifier when available, general location derived from your IP address, and information about ad impressions, clicks, and interactions.

Depending on your device settings, consent choices, and applicable law, Google may use this information to provide personalized or non-personalized advertisements, measure advertising effectiveness, prevent fraud and abuse, and maintain its advertising services.

If Lumi Hair asks for permission to track your activity across other companies’ apps or websites, you may allow or deny that request through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can later change your choice in your device’s privacy settings. Denying tracking permission does not necessarily prevent all advertising or ad measurement, but it limits access to the device advertising identifier and cross-app tracking.

We do not provide your email address, Hair Profile photos, generated previews, or Lumi Hair account identifier to Google for advertising.

Sale, sharing, and state privacy disclosures

We do not sell your personal information for money. We do not sell or share your photos, generated previews, email address, or account information for targeted advertising.

Depending on how applicable state law defines “sale,” “sharing,” or “targeted advertising,” the use of an advertising service such as Google AdMob may be treated as sharing information for cross-context behavioral advertising when personalized advertising or tracking is enabled.

You can limit this processing by denying App Tracking Transparency permission, adjusting advertising and privacy settings on your device, or contacting us at lumihair.help@outlook.com . We will not discriminate against you for exercising an applicable privacy right.

How long we keep information

Account information, hairstyle choices, Hair Profile photos, and generated previews generally remain associated with your account until you delete the account or the information is no longer reasonably necessary to provide Lumi Hair.

Replacing a Hair Profile photo may retain earlier private photo versions and previously generated previews. Individual deletion of a particular Hair Profile photo or generated preview is not currently available, but you may delete your account to remove the associated application data.

When you delete your account, we delete its private media and associated Lumi Hair application records from our active systems. Copies may remain temporarily in restricted backups, logs, or provider systems until they are overwritten or deleted under the applicable provider’s retention schedule.

OpenAI API content may be retained for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring as described above. Apple and RevenueCat may retain transaction and subscription records according to their respective legal obligations and retention policies. We may retain support communications, security records, and information required for legal, tax, accounting, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution purposes for as long as reasonably necessary.

Your choices and privacy requests

You may:

  • change supported account details within Lumi Hair;
  • remove saved hairstyles;
  • control camera, photo-library, and tracking permissions through your device settings;
  • delete your account through Profile → Delete Account; or
  • contact us for account-deletion assistance or another privacy request.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of your personal information. You may also have the right to opt out of certain advertising disclosures or appeal the denial of a privacy request.

To exercise an applicable right, contact lumihair.help@outlook.com . We may request information reasonably necessary to verify that you own the relevant account. Never send us your password.

Deleting your Lumi Hair account or uninstalling the app does not cancel an App Store subscription. You must separately manage or cancel the subscription through your Apple Account subscription settings.

Security

We use safeguards designed to protect information, including account authentication, access controls, encrypted network connections, and private media storage. However, no storage or transmission system can guarantee absolute security.

Processing locations

Pacai Technologies LLC and its service providers may process information in the United States and other countries where they or their subprocessors operate. Those countries may have privacy laws that differ from the laws where you live.

Adults only

Lumi Hair is intended only for people aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe that a person under 18 has provided personal information through Lumi Hair, contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

Our legal website

Our legal website is hosted through GitHub Pages. When you visit it, GitHub may automatically process technical and usage information such as your IP address, device and browser information, referral information, pages viewed, and the date and time of a request.

Pacai Technologies LLC does not place advertising, analytics scripts, or contact forms on this legal website.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will publish the updated policy with a revised effective date and provide additional notice when required by law. If a material change requires renewed consent, we will request it when appropriate.
